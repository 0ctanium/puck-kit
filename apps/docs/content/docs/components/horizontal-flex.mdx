**H Flex** (“Horizontal Flex”) lays its children out in a row, vertically centered, with a 16px gap. Children get a **Flex child** section (grow / shrink presets, align self, order).

## Tips

- Turn **wrap** on (Layout → Children) for rows of tags or logos.
- On Mobile, set the direction to vertical to stack the items.
