**Vimeo** embeds a video from a Vimeo link, including **unlisted links** with their private hash (`vimeo.com/123/abcdef` or `?h=`). It is 16:9 and full width by default, and sends Vimeo’s **do not track** flag (`dnt=1`) unless you turn privacy off.

## Props

Besides Puck’s `id`, the component stores these props (fields of the **Settings** tab, plus `tag`, `styles` and `advanced` in the **Style** tab):

  type={{
